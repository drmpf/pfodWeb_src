DECISION
========
Identical board.json chipGpios map to the plain Plus board (same 20
kept including the 3 LED tags, same 19 excluded) — populated-vs-
unpopulated IMU/mic doesn't change the keep/exclude decision, same
reasoning as Sense vs. base. This board's own pins_arduino.h needed the
SAME deliberate RX/TX-alias-deletion fix as the plain Plus variant (the
vendor source declares the identical bare RX/TX aliases) — see that
board's notes.txt for the full parser-collision writeup; applied
identically here and verified via the same rebuild-and-inspect method
(D6 -> plain GPIO, D7 -> plain GPIO + spi_ss).

NOT GUESSED: inherits the Plus board's fully-sourced findings verbatim,
since the underlying pin map and header exposure are provably identical
(byte-diff confirmed) — no new hardware research was needed.

SUMMARY: identical to Seeed_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus — 39 total Arduino pin
indices (0-38), 19 excluded (GPIO14-29, GPIO33-35), 20 kept available
(D0-D10, D11-D13/I2S, D17-D19/SPI1), 3 of which (GPIO11-13) additionally
carry onboard-LED-channel tags.
