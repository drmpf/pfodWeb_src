CONSIDERED BUT NOT CHANGED

- Button: no vendor documentation (GitHub README, product page, wiki,
  blog posts) mentions any onboard pushbutton/reset button on the CW01
  itself. XinaBox's xChip design philosophy keeps "core" modules like
  the CW01 minimal — buttons are supplied by separate xChips (e.g. a
  dedicated input xChip) connected over the xBUS I2C-style connector,
  not wired directly to this module's own GPIOs. No "button" capability
  tag was added anywhere.

- ADC / defaultRefVolts: no vendor schematic, wiki, or README was found
  describing a resistor-divider network on A0. In fact the CW01's own
  official pin table (GitHub README) does not list A0 among the module's
  documented/exposed pins at all — unlike NodeMCU/D1 Mini, which
  explicitly document a 3.3V-range divider on A0. Given no positive
  evidence either way, defaultRefVolts was left at the conservative
  inherited chip-level default of 1.0V (no board.json ADC override
  needed beyond the full-copy baseline).

- GPIO2 and GPIO0 chip-level pinNotes ("Commonly wired to the on-board
  LED"/"FLASH/BOOT button on dev boards") are generic chip-level
  boilerplate carried over from the base board.json; they do NOT apply
  to this specific board (this board's LEDs are on 5/12/13, and no
  button was confirmed at all) and were left as-is/unchanged since they
  are chip-level notes, not board-specific claims of a button/LED at
  those pins for the CW01.

- TX/RX check: GPIO1 (TX) and GPIO3 (RX) are hard-reserved by
  build_boards.js. The tagged LED pins (5, 12, 13) do not conflict with
  either.

SUMMARY

Created variants/esp8266/esp8266/xinabox/board.json (none existed
before) as a full copy of the esp8266 chip-level default, tagging
GPIO12/13/5 as a confirmed active-high discrete RGB LED
(led_r_high/led_g_high/led_b_high). No button tag added (no vendor
evidence of an onboard button). ADC defaultRefVolts left at the
inherited 1.0V chip default (no vendor evidence of a voltage divider on
A0; A0 isn't even documented as an exposed pin on this module).

[2026-07-12] Dedicated-hardware pin-exclusion audit (second, more
thorough pass) — re-verification + fresh sweep

CONTEXT: earlier in this same session, a network-wide contamination
sweep (across many boards, not specific to this audit) found and fixed
a real bug on this exact board: pins_arduino.h used to
`#include "../generic/common.h"`, and that shared file's default MISO
alias pointed at GPIO12 and MOSI at GPIO13 — exactly this board's
confirmed LED_RED (12) and LED_GREEN (13) channels. build_boards.js
(pfodWeb_src/build_boards.js, ~line 698-703) attaches spi_miso/spi_mosi
capabilities to whichever GPIO numbers match the parsed MISO/MOSI
consts, so both LED channels were silently getting bogus SPI
capability tags alongside their correct led_r_high/led_g_high tags.

1. RE-VERIFIED THE FIX IS IN PLACE AND CORRECT:
   - Read the full current variants/esp8266/esp8266/xinabox/pins_arduino.h.
   - Confirmed it no longer includes "../generic/common.h" — it is now
     self-contained.
   - Confirmed MISO and MOSI are not declared anywhere in the file (so
     build_boards.js's `consts.MISO`/`consts.MOSI` come back undefined
     and can never equal GPIO 12 or 13).
   - Confirmed SS=15, SCK=14, A0=17 were reproduced directly (these do
     not collide with GPIO5/12/13 or with each other's roles), and that
     LED_RED=12, LED_GREEN=13, LED_BUILTIN=5 are unchanged.
   - Confirmed board.json's chipGpios entries for "5", "12", "13" carry
     only {digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output, led_*_high} — no
     spi_ss/spi_mosi/spi_miso/spi_sck/i2c_sda/i2c_scl anywhere on those
     three pins. Traced build_boards.js's alias-capability logic
     (consts.sda=2, scl=14, ss=15, sck=14, mosi=undefined, miso=undefined)
     and confirmed none of those equal 5, 12, or 13, so no contamination
     path remains. Fix is CORRECT AND COMPLETE — no further change to
     pins_arduino.h was needed.
