4. https://github.com/OLIMEX/ESP8266/blob/master/HARDWARE/MOD-WIFI-ESP8266-DEV/
   MOD-WiFi-ESP8266-DEV%20revision%20B1/MOD-WiFi-ESP8266-DEV_Rev_B1.pdf
   Downloaded and read directly (PDF, 1-page schematic). Findings:
     - SW1 (SW-TAKTIT1185AU2 tactile switch) sits in the boot-mode-select
       resistor group alongside R7/R8/R9 (2k pull-ups to 3.3V on GPIO0,
       GPIO2, MTDO/GPIO15 respectively) and R11 (200R, in series with SW1
       to GND) — the classic "pull GPIO0 low via a button" flash-mode-entry
       circuit. R12 "NA(2k)" nearby is the separate optional GPIO4 I2C
       pull-up mentioned in Hardware_changes.txt, not part of the button.
     - LED/Green/0603 sits on the TXD net (which also drives the UEXT
       header's TXD pin), fed from U0TXD i.e. GPIO1. A second LED
       (LED/Red/0603, "PWR") is wired straight to 3.3V via R6 (2k) — pure
       power indicator, no GPIO involvement.
     - The 22-pin header's "ADC" pad (pad 12) traces from the ESP8266's
       TOUT pin (chip pin 6) with only C6 marked "NA" (unpopulated
       placeholder capacitor) on the line. No series/divider resistor pair
       visible anywhere near TOUT/ADC — unlike the 220k/100k dividers found
       on NodeMCU/D1 Mini/ESPino schematics already audited in this repo.

5. https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/292/DOC011292728.pdf

=== Considered but NOT tagged/changed, and why ===

- GPIO1 LED: real, vendor-schematic-confirmed onboard LED, BUT it is wired
  to GPIO1 which is this board's hardware UART0 TX0 pin (U0TXD) — used for
  the Serial pfod connection and hard-reserved as "serial_tx" in
  chipGpios. It functions as a TX-activity indicator, not a
  general-purpose GPIO-driven LED (directly analogous to the ESP-01's
  documented "TXD0" indicator in ../esp01/notes.txt — same reasoning
  applied). Per task instructions, build_boards.js hard-reserves TX/RX
  pins and silently discards any other capability tags placed on them, so
  no led_* tag was added to GPIO1.
- Power/PWR LED (red): hardwired directly to 3.3V via R6, not driven by
  any GPIO at all — not taggable by definition.
- R12 "NA(2k)" GPIO4 pad: an unpopulated optional I2C pull-up, not a
  button or LED — not relevant to either part of this task.
- ADC divider: schematic shows no divider resistors on the TOUT/ADC net
  (only an unpopulated placeholder cap, C6). Left defaultRefVolts at the
  inherited chip-level default of "1.0" (native 0-1.0V ADC range) since
  this -DEV breakout appears to expose the bare chip ADC pin.

=== Summary ===

Created variants/esp8266/esp8266/modwifi/board.json (none existed before,
now a full copy of the esp8266 chip-level default) tagging GPIO0 with
"button" (SW1, schematic- and vendor-doc-confirmed flash/UART-mode boot
button, pulled up 2k / pulled to GND via 200R when pressed — same pattern
as the espino board's GPIO0 button already in this repo). No LED tag added
(the board's one LED sits on the hard-reserved TX0/GPIO1 pin). ADC
defaultRefVolts left unchanged at 1.0V — no divider found in the vendor
schematic.

=== 2026-07-12: Full dedicated-hardware pin-exclusion sweep ===

Scope of this pass (distinct from the button/LED/ADC pass above): re-check
for ANY other onboard dedicated-hardware peripheral (SD card, display,
relay, sensor, etc.) not yet covered, and specifically verify the
already-tagged GPIO0 button pin isn't ALSO aliased as SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/
MISO/SCK in a way that could silently contaminate its capability list.

Sources consulted:

1. variants/esp8266/esp8266/modwifi/pins_arduino.h — read in full (41
   lines). Only declares PIN_WIRE_SDA=2, PIN_WIRE_SCL=14, LED_BUILTIN=1,
   and includes ../generic/common.h. No other pin aliases present.

2. variants/esp8266/esp8266/generic/common.h — read in full. Declares
   PIN_SPI_SS=15, PIN_SPI_MOSI=13, PIN_SPI_MISO=12, PIN_SPI_SCK=14, plus
   PIN_A0=17. No reference to GPIO0 anywhere in this file.

3. https://www.olimex.com/Products/IoT/ESP8266/MOD-WIFI-ESP8266-DEV/open-source-hardware
   (re-fetched). Full onboard component inventory: Power LED,
   user-programmable LED, UART-mode button (rev B+), UEXT pads, U.FL
   antenna pads, PCB antenna, 2MB SPI flash, 22-pin 0.1" header, SMT boot-
   mode jumpers. No SD card, display, relay, or sensor listed. Page
   explicitly points users wanting a relay/more integrated peripherals to
   the separate ESP8266-EVB board instead — confirming MOD-WIFI-ESP8266-DEV
   itself carries none.

4. https://github.com/OLIMEX/ESP8266/blob/master/HARDWARE/MOD-WIFI-ESP8266-DEV/Hardware_changes.txt
   (re-fetched, all revisions A, B, B1). Rev B adds only the button
   (+R11=200R) and the unpopulated GPIO4 I2C pull-up pads; rev B1 only
   changes crystal load capacitors C1/C2. No revision ever added an SD
   card, display, relay, or sensor.

Reasoning / findings:

- Alias-collision check (the critical item for this pass): GPIO0 does not
  appear in pins_arduino.h or generic/common.h at all — it is not aliased
  to SDA (=2), SCL (=14), SS (=15), MOSI (=13), MISO (=12), or SCK (=14).
  The "button" tag on GPIO0 in board.json's chipGpios was added directly
  as a manual override, entirely independent of the pins_arduino.h alias
  parser, so there is no contamination path here — confirmed clean.
- Other onboard peripherals: none found beyond what the prior pass already
  covered (power LED, TX0-activity LED, UART-mode button). Both the vendor
  product page and the full hardware-revision changelog were re-checked
  specifically for SD card / display / relay / sensor hardware and none
  exists on this board.
- SPI flash (GPIO6-11): already generically excluded by this repo's
  chip-level board.json comment ("GPIOs 6-11 are internal flash SPI and
  are never broken out on a standard (non-8285) module — intentionally
  omitted here") and isFlashInterfacePin() in common.h. Nothing
  board-specific to add.

Summary: NO CHANGE. variants/esp8266/esp8266/modwifi/board.json and
pins_arduino.h are unmodified by this pass. The full dedicated-hardware
sweep found no additional onboard peripherals to exclude, and the GPIO0
button tag is confirmed free of any SDA/SCL/SPI alias collision.
