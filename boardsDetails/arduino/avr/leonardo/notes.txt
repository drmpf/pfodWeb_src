Board: Arduino Leonardo (avr/leonardo)
Date: 2026-07-14

Sources consulted:
- docs.arduino.cc LED_BUILTIN language reference (official) — "On the
  Leonardo, the built-in LED is connected to pin 13... Setting the pin
  to HIGH value turns the LED on".

Per-pin findings:
- D13 (LED_BUILTIN) — confirmed onboard status LED, active-high. Not on
  this 32U4 pin map's SPI bus (SPI moved to D14-D17 on this family), so
  no dual-role caveat needed. Tagged
  ["digital_output","pwm_output","led_high"].

Propagation note: this board.json is reached via the ancestor-fallback
mechanism by `micro` (no board.json of its own, #includes this file
directly) and by `yun`/`yunmini`/`chiwawa`/`one` one level further (all
#include leonardo's pins_arduino.h too, with only Serial1/Bridge-related
#defines added on top). Confirmed correct for all of them — same 32U4
D13 LED convention, no vendor deviates from it on the AVR side.
esplora/leonardoeth/LilyPadUSB were split into their own directories
because they genuinely differ (Esplora's hardware bears no resemblance
to a plain status LED at all; Leonardo ETH adds a dedicated onboard
Ethernet-CS pin; LilyPad USB runs at 3.3V) — see those board.json/
notes.txt files.

Summary: Leonardo (+ Micro/Yun family via fallback) — D13 onboard LED
confirmed and tagged active-high.
