DECISIONS
=========
TAGGED: GPIO17 (PIN_LED1) led_high, GPIO43 (PIN_LED2, vendor's own
"dual white LEDs" comment) led_high, GPIO18 (PIN_NEOPIXEL) led_neopixel,
GPIO5 (PIN_BUTTON1, Button A) button, GPIO11 (PIN_BUTTON2, Button B)
button.

EXCLUDED (capabilities: []): GPIO29/30/31/35 (PIN_SPI1_SCK/MOSI/SS/
MISO) — this board's SECOND SPI bus is entirely dedicated to the onboard
1.3" TFT display (SS1=31 matches PIN_TFT_CS(31) exactly), unlike the
Seeed XIAO nRF52840 Plus's I2S/SPI1 pins which have no onboard consumer
and stay general — a genuinely different situation despite superficial
"second SPI-shaped bus" similarity, correctly NOT treated the same way.
GPIO35 also doubles as PIN_PDM_DIN (the onboard mic's data line,
#define-only) — consistent, both uses are non-general.

KEPT available: A0-A7 (GPIO21-28), default SPI bus (GPIO13-16), Wire bus
(GPIO19-20, shared by the onboard BMP280 pressure, SHT30 humidity/temp,
APDS9960 gesture/color, and LSM6DS33+LIS3MDL IMU sensors — kept
general-purpose per this repo's established "general bus, even with
onboard devices on it" policy, matching the Feather nRF52840 Sense's
identical situation).

NOT EXPOSED: PDM mic (GPIO36, the CLK line — DIN already excluded
above as part of SPI1), buzzer (GPIO46), QSPI flash (GPIO37-42), TFT
DC/RST/LITE (GPIO32-34) — all #define-only.

NOT GUESSED: LED/button/NeoPixel identity from unambiguous vendor
macros; SPI1-vs-TFT dedication confirmed by the exact SS1/PIN_TFT_CS
pin-number match.

SUMMARY: 47 total Arduino pin indices (0-46); 5 tagged (2 LED, 1
NeoPixel, 2 button); 4 excluded (onboard TFT's dedicated SPI1 bus);
A0-A7/default-SPI/Wire (including the sensor-sharing I2C bus) kept
general-purpose; mic-CLK/buzzer/flash/TFT-control-lines never appear
(unaliased #define-only).

POST-REVIEW RE-CHECK (2026-09-13, same day, user-requested — "recheck
pins against adafruit website docs"): downloaded and viewed the full
official pinout diagram directly (cdn-learn.adafruit.com/.../
Adafruit_nRF52840_CLUE_pinout.png). CONFIRMED every existing decision
exactly: L(red LED)=17/P1.01, NEOPIXEL=18/P0.16, WHITE_LEDS=43/P0.10
(matching this board's own "dual white LEDs" PIN_LED2), BUTTON_A=5/
P1.02, BUTTON_B=11/P1.10, SDA=20/P0.24, SCL=19/P0.25, MICROPHONE_DATA=
35/P0.00, MICROPHONE_CLOCK=36/P0.01, SPEAKER=46/P1.00, TFT_CS=31/P0.12,
TFT_DC=32/P0.13, TFT_SCK=29/P0.14, TFT_MOSI=30/P0.15, TFT_RESET=33/
P1.03, TFT_BACKLIGHT=34/P1.05 — all matching this board's own
variant.h/board.json indices exactly.

The diagram ALSO shows two real, physically-present pins that have NO
macro anywhere in this board's OWN variant.h at all — confirmed via a
direct grep against the vendor source, ruling out a transcription gap
on my part: PROXIMITY_LIGHT_INTERRUPT (index 44, physical P0.09 — the
same silicon pin also used for NFC1) and ACCELEROMETER_GYRO_INTERRUPT
(index 45, P1.06 — the onboard LSM6DS33's interrupt line). Per the
user's explicit instruction this pass to disregard CircuitPython (the
diagram mixes Arduino-index and CircuitPython/board-pin naming, and
these two labels most likely come from CircuitPython's own `board.py`
pin definitions, which this Arduino-only BSP simply never exposed as a
named macro at all) — since neither is aliased via `static const
uint8_t` in this board's own variant.h, BOTH remain entirely invisible
to this repo's parser by default, exactly like every other confirmed-
but-unaliased dedicated pin in this family. No board.json change
needed; recorded here purely for documentation completeness so a
future reviewer who spots these two labels on the diagram knows they
were found and deliberately not acted on (not missed).
