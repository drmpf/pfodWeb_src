PER-PIN REASONING
------------------
GPIO25 (PIN_LED):
  - Board's only onboard visual indicator. No NeoPixel/WS2812 exists on this board — confirmed by absence
    of any WS2812/RGB component in the schematic (only a single 2-pin Led1 component) and by the product
    page mentioning only one "User LED".
  - Polarity: ACTIVE HIGH. Schematic shows GPIO25 driving through series resistor R19 (470R) into Led1,
    which returns to GND — a GPIO-sources-current-into-anode topology, matching the standard Raspberry Pi
    Pico GPIO25 LED convention (this board is explicitly Pico-header/pinout compatible) and identical to
    the confirmed-active-high RP2040-Plus sibling's schematic (same R19/Led1 designators and values).
  - Applied capabilities: ["digital_output", "pwm_output", "led_high"] - digital_input dropped (matches
    established led_high precedent for dedicated onboard LED pins, e.g.
    ../../rp2040/waveshare_rp2040_plus/board.json GPIO25, ../rpipico2/board.json). pwm_output retained
    since arduino-pico's analogWrite() works on any RP2040/RP2350 GPIO, enabling LED brightness dimming.

BOOT / RESET buttons (Key1/Key2 in schematic):
  - Both out of scope for capability tagging: Key1 (reset, RUN net) and Key2 (BOOTSEL, QSPI_SS_N net) are
    not represented by any alias in pins_arduino.h and are not readable via a normal GPIO digitalRead() -
    same rationale already established for the RP2040 sibling and other rpipico-family boards in this
    repo. No chipGpios entry needed/possible for either.

Bus-collision check (per the audit's known bug class): GPIO25 carries no SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK alias
in this board's pins_arduino.h — SPI0 uses GPIO16 (MISO)/17 (SS)/18 (SCK)/19 (MOSI), SPI1 uses GPIO12
(MISO)/13 (SS)/14 (SCK)/15 (MOSI), Wire0 uses GPIO8 (SDA)/9 (SCL), Wire1 uses GPIO6 (SDA)/7 (SCL). No
collision, no pins_arduino.h edit needed.
