THE PRECEDENT CHAIN (made explicit, not self-referential)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Primary/root source: the OFFICIAL Raspberry Pi pico-sdk repository board

   boards/include/boards/pico2.h — defines `PICO_DEFAULT_LED_PIN 25` with
   NO `PICO_DEFAULT_LED_PIN_INVERTED` override (that override only appears

   Tutorials, SunFounder, Coxxect, Keyestudio) all stating GP25 HIGH = LED
