PER-PIN REASONING
------------------
GPIO25 (PIN_LED / LED_BUILTIN, "STAT LED"):
  CONFIRMED real, populated, active-high onboard LED (see schematic
  finding #8 above). Added chipGpios entry:
    "25": { "capabilities": ["digital_output","pwm_output","led_high"], ... }
  digital_input dropped (dedicated LED pin, not a general input) and
  pwm_output kept (arduino-pico analogWrite() works on any RP2040 GPIO),
  matching the rpipico/wiznet_5500_evb_pico precedent exactly. Without
  this entry GPIO25 would not appear in the Designer pin list at all
  (common.h's shared fallback deliberately excludes it, and PIN_LED /
  LED_BUILTIN are both bare #defines that the parser does not harvest).

GPIO31 (PIN_NEOPIXEL / PIN_WIRE1_SDA / PIN_WIRE1_SCL):
  CONFIRMED NOT a real, populated NeoPixel or second I2C bus on this
  board — NUM_NEOPIXEL(0) in the board's own header, no NeoPixel/WS2812
  anywhere in SparkFun's hookup guide or official schematic, and 31 is
  not even a real RP2040 GPIO (chip only has GPIO0-29; confirmed absent
  from the RP2040 IC schematic symbol). Both PIN_WIRE1_SDA and
  PIN_WIRE1_SCL also point at the same "31" value, which alone rules out
  a genuine second I2C bus (SDA and SCL cannot be the same physical pin).
  NO chipGpios entry added for GPIO31 — and this is deliberate, not an
  oversight: none of PIN_NEOPIXEL/PIN_WIRE1_SDA/PIN_WIRE1_SCL are ever
  referenced by a `static const uint8_t` alias in this board's headers
  (they're bare #defines only), so build_boards.js's parser already never
  adds GPIO31 to the generated pin list. Adding a chipGpios entry here
  (even an empty-capabilities one) would be the one action that could
  wrongly introduce a nonexistent "GPIO31" pin into the Designer, since
  chipGpios keys are unconditionally unioned into the pin list
  (buildEsp32Board(), allGpios union step). Left untouched.

Onboard button: none beyond the standard RP2040 BOOTSEL (not a normal
GPIO — out of scope, same as every other RP2040 board in this repo).
Confirmed no separate general-purpose button exists via the hookup guide
and schematic.

SUMMARY
-------
Added one chipGpios override: GPIO25 tagged led_high (STAT LED, confirmed
active-high via official schematic). GPIO31 (PIN_NEOPIXEL/PIN_WIRE1_SDA/
PIN_WIRE1_SCL) confirmed NOT a real onboard NeoPixel or second I2C bus —
correctly left with no chipGpios entry, matching its current (correct)
total absence from the generated Designer pin list.
