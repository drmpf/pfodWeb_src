PER-PIN REASONING
------------------
GPIO25 (PIN_LED):
  - Board's only onboard visual indicator. No NeoPixel/WS2812 exists on this board (unlike the "Zero"
    form-factor sibling waveshare_rp2040_zero, which has a NeoPixel instead of a plain LED) - confirmed by
    absence of any WS2812/NeoPixel component in the schematic and absence of any such mention in Waveshare's
    docs/wiki for this specific board.
  - Polarity: ACTIVE HIGH. Primary confirmation is Zephyr's rp2040_plus.dts GPIO_ACTIVE_HIGH flag (hardware-
    verified independent source). This also matches the standard Raspberry Pi Pico GPIO25 LED convention
    (this board is explicitly Pico-header/pinout compatible per Waveshare's own description), and is
    consistent with the schematic's Led1/R19 series arrangement between GPIO25 and GND.
  - Applied capabilities: ["digital_output", "pwm_output", "led_high"] - digital_input dropped (matches
    established led_high precedent for dedicated onboard LED pins, e.g. ../rpipico/board.json GPIO25,
    esp32/esp32c5/XIAO_ESP32C5 GPIO27). pwm_output retained since arduino-pico's analogWrite() works on any
    RP2040 GPIO (all 8 PWM slices cover GPIO0-29), enabling LED brightness dimming.

BOOT / RESET buttons (Key1/Key2 in schematic):
  - Both out of scope for capability tagging: Key1 (reset, RUN net) and Key2 (BOOTSEL, QSPI_SS_N net) are
    not represented by any alias in pins_arduino.h and are not readable via a normal GPIO digitalRead() -
    same rationale already established in ../rpipico/board.json and ../waveshare_rp2040_zero/board.json.
    No chipGpios entry needed/possible for either.

Bus-collision check (per the audit's known bug class): GPIO25 carries no SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK alias in
this board's pins_arduino.h (SPI0/SPI1 use GPIO12-19, Wire0/Wire1 use GPIO6-9) - no collision, no
pins_arduino.h edit needed.
