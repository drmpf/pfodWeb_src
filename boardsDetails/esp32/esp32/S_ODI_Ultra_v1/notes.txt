  carrying only generic capabilities (analog_input_serial/digital_input/
  digital_output/pwm_output/dac_output as applicable per pin, per the

  exclusion findings of any kind. GPIOs 6-11 (flash) and 16-17 (PSRAM) are
  absent from chipGpios chip-wide (not board-specific); GPIOs 34-39 are
  input-only.

Summary: This board has no board-specific board.json - it uses the shared
chip-level default at variants/esp32/esp32/board.json unchanged, identical
to sibling boards that also fall back to the same ancestor (e.g.
BharatPi-A7672S-4G, ET-Board, Microduino-esp32). No board-specific
button/LED/exclusion research has been done for this board; none was
