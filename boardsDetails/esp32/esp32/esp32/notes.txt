Summary: This is the chip-level "esp32" variant itself (boardName
"ESP32", displayName "ESP32 Dev Module") - it has no board-specific
board.json and uses its own chip-level default directly. It is identical
in tagging to sibling boards under variants/esp32/esp32/ that likewise
have no board.json of their own and also fall back to this same
chip-level default (e.g. doitESP32devkitV1, esp32-sbc-fabgl, esp320,
esp32vn-iot-uno, firebeetle32, frog32).
