Board: deneyapkart (Deneyap Kart / ESP32)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill - findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- Vendor pins_arduino.h - cited directly for the dedicated camera, IMU
  I2C, and mic pin aliases (CAMSD/CAMSC/CAMD2-9/CAMPC/CAMXC/CAMH/CAMV,
  IMUSD/IMUSC, MICD/MICC).
- docs.deneyapkart.org "Pin Tanimlamalari" (DYDK) - cited directly as
  confirming GPIO0 is the onboard GPKEY button: 10K pull-up to 3.3V,
  pressed pulls LOW, also used for manual boot mode entry.
- mekinfo.net Deneyap Kart setup guide - cited directly as confirming
  LEDG (GPIO1) and LEDR (GPIO3), and also LEDB (GPIO4), are active-low.
- build_boards.js's own serial-pin reservation behavior - cited as the
  reason GPIO1/GPIO3 were not tagged despite the confirmed LED evidence.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO15, GPIO18, GPIO19, GPIO21, GPIO22, GPIO23, GPIO25,
  GPIO26, GPIO32, GPIO33, GPIO34, GPIO35, GPIO36, GPIO39: excluded
  (capabilities: [] / omitted from chipGpios) - dedicated onboard camera,
  IMU I2C, and mic pins (vendor pins_arduino.h CAMSD/CAMSC/CAMD2-9/CAMPC/
  CAMXC/CAMH/CAMV, IMUSD/IMUSC, MICD/MICC).
- GPIO0: tagged ["digital_input", "button"] - confirmed onboard GPKEY
  button (docs.deneyapkart.org Pin Tanimlamalari DYDK: 10K pull-up to
  3.3V, pressed pulls LOW, also used for manual boot mode entry).
- GPIO1 (LEDG), GPIO3 (LEDR): confirmed active-low per mekinfo.net Deneyap
  Kart setup guide, but deliberately NOT tagged led_g_low/led_r_low -
  they are also this board's primary serial TX/RX pins, which
  build_boards.js hard-reserves for serial duty (pfodWeb's serial
  connection needs them), so any chipGpios tag on them would be silently
  discarded in the generated output.
- GPIO4 (LEDB): also confirmed active-low, but already excluded above as
  a dedicated IMU I2C SDA pin - not reachable for LED tagging either way.
- GPIO2, GPIO14, GPIO27, GPIO37, GPIO38: kept, unchanged generic
  capabilities.

Summary: Camera/IMU/mic pins excluded per vendor pins_arduino.h; GPIO0
tagged button (confirmed onboard GPKEY button, also used for manual boot
entry, per docs.deneyapkart.org); GPIO1/GPIO3 (LEDG/LEDR, confirmed
active-low) deliberately left untagged because they double as this
board's hard-reserved serial TX/RX pins and any tag would be silently
discarded by build_boards.js; GPIO4 (LEDB, also active-low) already
excluded as a dedicated IMU I2C pin.
