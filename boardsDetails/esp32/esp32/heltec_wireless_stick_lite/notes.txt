1) SOURCES CONSULTED

- https://resource.heltec.cn/download/Wireless_Stick_Lite/Wireless_Stick_Lite.pdf
  Official Heltec "Wireless Stick Lite Pinout Diagram" PDF. Fetched and read directly
  (text/labels extracted from the PDF). CONFIRMS:
    * GPIO0 is labeled "PRG_BUTTON" (yellow "Control" category in the diagram legend),
      alongside ADC2_1/TOUCH1.
    * GPIO25 is labeled "LED" (yellow "Control" category in the diagram legend),
      alongside ADC2_8/DAC2.
  This is the primary/strongest piece of evidence - an official vendor schematic-derived
  pinout diagram, not just a header-file alias name.

- https://github.com/HelTecAutomation/Heltec_ESP32/blob/master/examples/Factory_Test/Wireless_Stick_Lite_FactoryTest/Wireless_Stick_Lite_FactoryTest.ino
  (also fetched raw via
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/HelTecAutomation/Heltec_ESP32/master/examples/Factory_Test/Wireless_Stick_Lite_FactoryTest/Wireless_Stick_Lite_FactoryTest.ino)
  Official Heltec factory-test firmware for this exact board. CONFIRMS:
    * `attachInterrupt(0, interrupt_GPIO0, FALLING); ... if (digitalRead(0)==0)` -
      GPIO0 is read as a real button input, active LOW (pressed = LOW), consistent
      with a button pulled HIGH and grounded on press - i.e. a genuine physical button,
      not just a strap pin being exercised.
    * `pinMode(LED, OUTPUT); digitalWrite(LED, LOW);` at setup (LED starts off), then
      `digitalWrite(LED, HIGH);` when a packet is received (rxNumber%2==0) - i.e. HIGH
      turns the LED on. `LED` resolves to GPIO25 per pins_arduino.h. This confirms
      GPIO25 drives a real onboard single-color LED, and that it is ACTIVE-HIGH
      (HIGH = on, LOW = off).

2) PINS CONSIDERED BUT NOT TAGGED

- No other candidate GPIOs were considered. Only GPIO0 (button) and GPIO25 (LED) had
  any alias suggesting a user-facing control, and both were independently confirmed
  by the vendor pinout diagram plus the vendor factory-test firmware. No ambiguity
  remained on either pin's identity or polarity, so no best-guess judgment calls were
  needed for these two.
- TX/RX check: this board's TX=GPIO1, RX=GPIO3 (from pins_arduino.h). Neither GPIO0
  nor GPIO25 coincides with TX/RX, so no TX/RX-conflict exclusion applies here.
- GPIO26 (LoRa_DIO0/DAC1 per the vendor PDF) was noted in the diagram as adjacent to
  the LED pin but is a dedicated onboard LoRa control line (already excluded from
  chipGpios per the pre-existing pfodWeb NOTE at the top of board.json) - left alone,
  not reconsidered.

3) SUMMARY OF WHAT WAS TAGGED

- GPIO0: capabilities set to exactly ["digital_input","button"] - confirmed onboard
  PRG_BUTTON (active LOW) via official Heltec pinout PDF + factory-test firmware.
- GPIO25: capabilities set to exactly ["digital_output","led_high","pwm_output"]
  (pwm_output retained because it was already present on this pin) - confirmed
  onboard single-color LED, active-high (HIGH=on), via official Heltec pinout PDF +
  factory-test firmware.
