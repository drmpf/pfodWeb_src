Board: deneyapkart1A (Deneyap Kart 1A / ESP32)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill - findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- Vendor pins_arduino.h - cited directly for the dedicated camera and
  microSD card pin aliases (CAMSD/CAMSC/CAMD2-9/CAMPC/CAMXC/CAMH/CAMV,
  SDMI/SDMO/SDCS/SDCK).
- https://deneyapkart.org/en/docs/deneyap-kart-1a - cited directly as
  confirming GPIO0 is the onboard "General Purpose Button" on D8, and
  GPIO13 is the onboard "addressable RGB LED (Pin: D12)".
- This board's own vendor pins_arduino.h LED_BUILTIN/RGB_BUILTIN alias -
  cited as matching the GPIO13 NeoPixel finding.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO2, GPIO5, GPIO12, GPIO14, GPIO18, GPIO19, GPIO21, GPIO22, GPIO23,
  GPIO25, GPIO26, GPIO27, GPIO32, GPIO33, GPIO34, GPIO35, GPIO36, GPIO39:
  excluded (capabilities: [] / omitted from chipGpios) - dedicated onboard
  camera and microSD card pins (vendor pins_arduino.h CAMSD/CAMSC/
  CAMD2-9/CAMPC/CAMXC/CAMH/CAMV, SDMI/SDMO/SDCS/SDCK).
- GPIO0: tagged ["digital_input", "button"] - confirmed onboard "General
  Purpose Button" on D8 (deneyapkart.org/en/docs/deneyap-kart-1a).
- GPIO13: tagged ["digital_output", "pwm_output", "led_neopixel"] -
  confirmed onboard "addressable RGB LED (Pin: D12)"
  (deneyapkart.org/en/docs/deneyap-kart-1a), matching vendor
  pins_arduino.h LED_BUILTIN/RGB_BUILTIN alias.
- GPIO1, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO15, GPIO37, GPIO38: kept, unchanged generic
  capabilities.

Summary: Camera and microSD pins excluded per vendor pins_arduino.h;
GPIO0 tagged button (confirmed onboard "General Purpose Button", per
deneyapkart.org's own docs); GPIO13 tagged led_neopixel (confirmed
onboard addressable RGB LED, matching vendor LED_BUILTIN/RGB_BUILTIN
alias).
