m5stack_paper - button/LED research notes (2026-07-11)

Sources consulted:
- https://github.com/m5stack/M5EPD (raw src/M5EPD.h) - confirms #define
  M5EPD_KEY_LEFT_PIN 37, M5EPD_KEY_PUSH_PIN 38, M5EPD_KEY_RIGHT_PIN 39.
  These are the three GPIOs wired to the physical dial/rotary switch
  (left / push-center-power / right).
- https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/m5paper_v1.1 (pinmap section) -
  independently confirms G37/G38/G39 = Right/Middle(Power)/Left rotary
  switch positions (note: left/right labels are swapped vs the library
  header naming, but both agree these three GPIOs are the physical
  buttons - direction naming discrepancy doesn't affect the tag, which
  is direction-agnostic "button").
- https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/m5paper (general Paper page) -
  confirms 3 physical buttons via M5.BtnA/BtnB/BtnC (M5Unified) and
  states no status LED (single-color or RGB/neopixel) is present on
  the device.
- WebSearch results referencing OpenELAB Wiki M5Paper page and
  m5-docs GitHub markdown - corroborated the GPIO37/38/39 button
  assignment (used only to cross-check, not directly cited/fetched).

Pins considered and NOT tagged:
- No LED candidate found at all - M5Paper (E-ink display device) has
  no onboard status LED, single-color or NeoPixel, per official docs.
  This is a confirmed absence, not a guess - no LED tag applied
  anywhere.
- GPIO36 (E-ink touch controller interrupt line) - already excluded
  from chipGpios by the prior pin-exclusion audit pass; not part of
  this task and left untouched.
- No TX/RX conflicts encountered: board TX=1, RX=3 (from
  pins_arduino.h), neither overlaps GPIO37/38/39, so all three button
  tags apply cleanly with no silent-discard risk.

What was tagged:
- GPIO37, GPIO38, GPIO39 each changed from
  ["analog_input","digital_input"] to ["digital_input","button"] in
  board.json - confirmed as the three M5Paper rotary/dial-switch
  physical buttons (M5EPD_KEY_LEFT_PIN/M5EPD_KEY_PUSH_PIN/
  M5EPD_KEY_RIGHT_PIN) via M5EPD library source and docs.m5stack.com
  pinmap.
