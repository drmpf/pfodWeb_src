- April Brother's official ESPea32 schematic, ESPea32-schematic.pdf
  (github.com/AprilBrother/ESPea32/hardware).

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO5: tagged "led_low" (capabilities: ["digital_output", "led_low",
  "pwm_output"]). Note from board.json: confirmed onboard RED LED (LED1)
  wired VDD3V3 -> R1(470R) -> LED1 anode -> cathode -> GPIO5 in April
  Brother's official ESPea32-schematic.pdf
  (github.com/AprilBrother/ESPea32/hardware); GPIO5 sinks current to
  light it, so it is active-low (LOW = on). Matches LED_BUILTIN alias.
- GPIO0: tagged "button" (capabilities: ["digital_input", "button"]).
  Note from board.json: confirmed onboard FLASH pushbutton (K2) wired
  GPIO0 to GND in the same schematic; matches KEY_BUILTIN alias in
  pins_arduino.h.
- All remaining chipGpios entries (1,2,3,4,12,13,14,15,18,19,21,22,23,25,
  26,27,32,33,34,35,36,37,38,39) are unchanged from the esp32/esp32
  chip-level default (variants/esp32/esp32/board.json) - plain
  analog/digital/pwm/dac capabilities per pin, no additional
  board-specific findings.

Summary: GPIO5 tagged led_low as the onboard active-low RED LED (LED1),
and GPIO0 tagged button as the onboard FLASH pushbutton (K2), both
confirmed via April Brother's official ESPea32 schematic; all other pins
left at the chip-level esp32/esp32 default.
