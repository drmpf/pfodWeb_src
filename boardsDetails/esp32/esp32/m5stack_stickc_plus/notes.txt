Board: m5stack_stickc_plus (M5Stack StickC Plus), chip esp32
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill - findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- Vendor pins_arduino.h for this board (checked for GPIO21/22 aliases;
  neither is aliased anywhere in this board's own pins_arduino.h).
- M5Stack official docs: "IMU (MPU6886) and PMU (AXP192)... SCL on GPIO22
  and SDA on GPIO21" - confirms the dedicated internal I2C bus to the
  onboard MPU6886 IMU and AXP192 PMU.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 12, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 33, 34,
  35, 36, 37, 38, 39: left at the plain chip-level default capability set
  (no board-specific note).
- GPIO21, 22: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated internal
  I2C bus to the onboard MPU6886 IMU and AXP192 PMU, confirmed via M5Stack
  official docs ("IMU (MPU6886) and PMU (AXP192)... SCL on GPIO22 and SDA on
  GPIO21"). This is separate from this board's exposed Grove I2C bus at
  SDA=32/SCL=33, which stays available (untouched/kept in chipGpios).

Note: this board.json is byte-identical to the sibling m5stack_stickc and
m5stack_stickc_plus2 board.json files (each has its own board.json file at
its own directory - none of the three relies on ancestor-fallback - but the
audited findings and resulting capability sets happen to be the same across
all three StickC-family boards, since they share the same MPU6886/AXP192
internal I2C wiring).

Summary: GPIO21/22 excluded as the dedicated internal I2C bus to the onboard
MPU6886 IMU and AXP192 PMU (confirmed via M5Stack official docs); the
board's separate Grove I2C bus (GPIO32/33) remains available; no button/LED
tags were applied on this board.
