lolin_c3_pico board.json dedicated-hardware pin-exclusion audit research notes
================================================================================

Board: lolin_c3_pico (variants/esp32/esp32c3/lolin_c3_pico), chip esp32c3.
Real-world identity: Wemos/LOLIN C3 Pico.
pins_arduino.h declares PIN_RGB_LED=7 (with LED_BUILTIN/RGB_BUILTIN wired to
it via the SOC_GPIO_PIN_COUNT+N virtual-pin convention, RGB_BRIGHTNESS=64,
RGB_BUILTIN_LED_COLOR_ORDER=LED_COLOR_ORDER_RGB), plus TX=21/RX=20,
SDA=8/SCL=10, VBAT=3 with BAT_VOLT_PIN macro, SCK=1/MISO=0/MOSI=4/SS=5, and
A0-A5=0-5. No board.json override existed before this audit (used the
chip-level esp32c3/board.json default). Flagged by the discovery sweep for
the PIN_RGB_LED declaration plus the VBAT/BAT_VOLT_PIN pair, which the
sibling board lolin_c3_mini (same PIN_RGB_LED=7) does not have.

Sources consulted
------------------
- https://www.wemos.cc/en/latest/c3/c3_pico.html
  Official WEMOS product page (also the URL already cited at the top of
  this board's own pins_arduino.h). WebFetch summary confirms "1x WS2812
  RGB LED" and "1x Battery port with 500mA charging" among the feature
  bullets, but the page text itself does not give GPIO numbers or divider
  values - it links to the schematic and dimension PDFs.
- https://www.wemos.cc/en/latest/_static/files/sch_c3_pico_v1.0.0.pdf
  Official WEMOS schematic (KiCad export, "File: C3_pico.kicad_sch", single
  A4 sheet), fetched and read directly as a PDF image (WebFetch's text
  extraction failed on the compressed PDF content, so the saved PDF was
  re-read directly with the Read tool, which renders it as an image) - this
  is the ground-truth schematic, not a marketing rendering or a
  third-party guess. Findings:
    * U3 "WS2812B-0807" component (4-pin WS2812-style addressable RGB LED):
      pin DI (data-in, pin 4) wired to net "IO7", VDD to +3V3, GND to GND,
      DO unconnected. Confirms GPIO7 is a genuinely populated onboard
      NeoPixel data pin.
    * U4 "TP4054" (LiPo linear charge-management IC): VCC fed from VBUS via
      R9 (5.1k), CHRG output drives LED1 (red charge-status LED), BAT
      output net labelled "VBAT" feeds battery connector P3 (PH2.0,
      "P_01x02"). The VBAT net is also tapped by a resistor divider: R7
      (100k) in series from VBAT to node "AD_BAT", and R10 (100k) as a
      shunt from AD_BAT to GND, with C9 (100nF) filtering the AD_BAT node.
      AD_BAT is routed through JP1 (labelled "BAT_AD") to net "IO3".
      Confirms GPIO3 is a genuine battery-voltage-sense pin, fed by a
      100K/100K (1:1) resistive divider, gated by a jumper rather than a
      permanent hard-wire.
    * Two physical pushbuttons present: SW1 ("SW_3x4x2.5") wired on the EN
      (chip enable/reset) net, alongside R3 (10k pull-up to 3V3) and C14
      (1uF debounce/hold-up cap) - this is the hardware RESET button, not a
      GPIO, so it has no chipGpios entry. SW2 ("SW_3x4x2.5") wired on a
      second net alongside R4 (10k pull-up to 3V3); a third resistor R6
      (10k pull-up to 3V3, no switch attached) sits on a separate
      strapping-pin net. The PDF's embedded text extraction rendered the
      two adjacent net labels for R4/SW2 and R6 ambiguously in this
      session's tooling (the ESP32-C3's three strapping pins GPIO2/GPIO8/
      GPIO9 produced visually similar short label text) - this was NOT
      relied on alone; resolved using the independent source below.
- https://blog.corrlabs.com/2023/08/lolin-esp32-c3-pico-notes.html
  Independent third-party write-up by an owner of this exact board model,
  describing the DFU/flash-mode procedure using the board's own silkscreen
  button naming: "Hold on Button 9, Press Button Reset, Release Button 9
  when you hear the prompt tone on USB reconnection." This directly
  identifies the physical pushbutton (SW2 in the schematic) as wired to
  GPIO9 - the standard ESP32-C3 BOOT/download strapping pin - resolving
  the schematic-text-extraction ambiguity above without guessing. This is
  also consistent with general ESP32-C3 boot-mode documentation (GPIO9
  held low on reset enters the serial bootloader; internal weak pull-up
  otherwise) and with R6 (no switch) simply being a pull-up placed on a
  different strapping pin (GPIO8) to guarantee it reads HIGH at boot,
  matching the chip-level pinNotes entry "8": "Must be HIGH or floating on
  reboot" already present unchanged.
- https://mischianti.org/wemos-lolin-esp32-c3-pico-high-resolution-pinout-and-specs/
  Independent third-party pinout/specs reference. WebFetch summary
  corroborates "RGB_LED, driven by GPIO7" and confirms the presence of
  separate "Boot Button" and "Reset Button" (does not itself give the Boot
  button's GPIO number - used only as corroboration for the LED pin, not
  as the deciding source for the button).
- https://www.cnx-software.com/2022/10/28/lolin-c3-pico-esp32-c3-board-battery-charging-support/
  Independent news-aggregator coverage of the board's launch. WebFetch
  summary confirms "an RGB LED", a "Reset button and user button", and a
  "PH-2.0 Battery port with 500mA charging" - broadly corroborates the
  schematic findings but gives no GPIO numbers of its own.
- Sibling board precedent: variants/esp32/esp32c3/lolin_c3_mini/
  pins_arduino.h (already in this repo) - has the identical
  `#define PIN_RGB_LED 7` declaration but NO VBAT/BAT_VOLT_PIN lines and no
  battery-connector hardware. Used only to corroborate that GPIO7's
  NeoPixel behaviour is shared/consistent across the WEMOS C3 board family
  (same core schematic block), and to confirm the task's premise that the
  Pico variant genuinely differs from the Mini variant by having the
  LiPo-charging/VBAT-sense circuit - not used as evidence for the Pico's
  own VBAT circuit, which was independently confirmed via this board's own
  schematic above.
- variants/esp32/esp32c3/board.json (chip-level default, already in this
  repo) - starting point for the new per-board override; also the source
  of the pre-existing pinNotes entries for GPIO2/8/9 (unchanged by this
  audit).

Reasoning per pin
------------------
GPIO7 (PIN_RGB_LED) - CONFIRMED onboard WS2812/NeoPixel-style RGB LED. The
  vendor's own schematic shows a real WS2812B-0807 component with its DI
  (data-in) pin tied to the IO7 net, VDD to 3V3, GND to GND - not a guess,
  not a boilerplate name reused from another product, and matching this
  board's own pins_arduino.h PIN_RGB_LED=7 / RGB_BUILTIN_LED_COLOR_ORDER
  macros. Tagged capabilities ["digital_output","led_neopixel","pwm_output"]
  (pwm_output retained since it was already present at the chip-level
  default for this pin), matching this repo's led_neopixel precedent (e.g.
  waveshare_esp32_c3_zero GPIO10, adafruit_qtpy_esp32c3 GPIO2). Checked this
  board's own pins_arduino.h for any SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK/TX/RX alias
  landing on GPIO7: TX=21, RX=20, SDA=8, SCL=10, SCK=1, MISO=0, MOSI=4,
  SS=5 - none is 7, so no alias-conflict fix was needed for this pin.

GPIO3 (VBAT / BAT_VOLT_PIN) - CONFIRMED onboard battery-voltage-sense pin.
  The vendor's own schematic shows the TP4054 charger's BAT/VBAT output
  rail tapped by a 100k (R7, series) / 100k (R10, shunt-to-GND) resistive
  divider, filtered by C9, and routed through jumper JP1 ("BAT_AD") to net
  IO3 - matching this board's own pins_arduino.h `VBAT = 3` /
  `BAT_VOLT_PIN` declarations. Because the divider is gated by a jumper
  (JP1) rather than a permanent hard-wire, and GPIO3 doubles as a plain
  header analog pin (A3) when JP1 is left open, this pin was NOT excluded
  from chipGpios and was NOT given a restricted capability set - it keeps
  the chip-level default (["analog_input","digital_input","digital_output",
  "pwm_output"]), matching this repo's precedent for non-exclusive/
  jumper-gated battery-sense pins (e.g. esp32/d32 GPIO35, ttgo-t-oi-plus
  GPIO2, uPesy_esp32c3_mini/basic GPIO0). Per the task's instruction, the
  corrected ADC reference was computed from the confirmed divider ratio:
  3.3V * (R_series + R_shunt) / R_shunt = 3.3V * (100000+100000)/100000 =
  6.6V. This value was recorded in a "note" field on the GPIO3 chipGpios
  entry (not applied to the board-wide "adc.defaultRefVolts", since that
  field is global and this board has five other unrelated analog pins
  (A0/A1/A2/A4/A5 = GPIO0/1/2/4/5) that do NOT have this divider - a
  board-wide override would have silently mis-scaled all of them, unlike
  the wiolink/esp8266 precedent where the divider sits on the board's only
  analog pin). Checked for SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK/TX/RX alias collisions
  on GPIO3: none found (same alias list as above) - only the pre-existing,
  non-bus A3=3 placeholder alias, which is consistent with (not a conflict
  with) the confirmed VBAT/analog function.

GPIO9 (opportunistic section 7a button check) - CONFIRMED onboard physical
  BOOT/download pushbutton. The schematic shows SW2 (SW_3x4x2.5) with a
  10k pull-up (R4) on a GPIO net, and an independent board-owner write-up
  (corrlabs.com) names the physical button used for entering flash mode as
  "Button 9", directly identifying that net as GPIO9 - the standard
  ESP32-C3 BOOT/download strapping pin (chip-level pinNotes "Must be HIGH
  for normal boot" retained unchanged, not removed - expected for this
  pin). Tagged capabilities ["digital_input","button"] only, per this
  repo's exclusion rule for button pins (no analog/output/pwm mixed in).
  Checked this board's own pins_arduino.h for any alias landing on GPIO9:
  none found (this board's pins_arduino.h never declares GPIO9 at all,
  unlike some sibling boards where a boilerplate SCL/SS alias collided
  with the button pin) - so no pins_arduino.h edit was required for this
  pin either.

Alias-conflict check (per this session's playbook step)
-----------------------------------------------------------
This board's own pins_arduino.h declares TX=21, RX=20, SDA=8, SCL=10,
SCK=1, MISO=0, MOSI=4, SS=5. None of these constants equal 7, 3, or 9 - so
no SDA/SCL/SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK/TX/RX alias collides with the confirmed RGB LED
(GPIO7), battery-sense pin (GPIO3), or BOOT button (GPIO9). Unlike several
sibling boards audited earlier this session (e.g. esp32c3-devkit-lipo,
waveshare_esp32_c3_zero, both of which had a boilerplate SCL alias
colliding with their confirmed button pin), this board's pins_arduino.h
required NO edits - it was read in full and no colliding alias was found,
so nothing was deleted.

Pins considered but NOT changed
---------------------------------
- GPIO8 (SDA=8) and GPIO10 (SCL=10): left at the chip-level default. The
  schematic's R6 pull-up sits on GPIO8 (a strapping pin, no switch
  attached) - this is a plain "hold HIGH at boot" pull-up, not a button or
  other dedicated function, and is already covered by the existing
  chip-level pinNotes entry for GPIO8. No dedicated function was found for
  GPIO10 either.
- All other GPIOs (0, 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 11, 20, 21): left at the chip-level
  esp32c3/board.json default. No dedicated-hardware declarations found for
  any of them in this board's pins_arduino.h, and no vendor documentation
  surfaced during research suggesting any other dedicated onboard
  peripheral.

What was tagged/changed
-------------------------
1. Created variants/esp32/esp32c3/lolin_c3_pico/board.json (none existed
   before this audit) as a full copy of the chip-level esp32c3/board.json
   default, with:
     - GPIO7 capabilities changed to
       ["digital_output","led_neopixel","pwm_output"] (was
       ["digital_input","digital_output","pwm_output"]) - confirmed
       onboard WS2812 RGB LED.
     - GPIO9 capabilities changed to ["digital_input","button"] (was
       ["digital_input","digital_output","pwm_output"]) - confirmed
       onboard BOOT button (opportunistic check).
     - GPIO3 capabilities left unchanged (chip-level default), but a
       "note" field was added documenting the confirmed 100K/100K VBAT
       divider and the computed corrected reference (6.6V).
   plus a pfodWeb NOTE comment block at the top of the file citing the
   sources above.
2. pins_arduino.h: read in full; no alias-conflict edits were required
   (see alias-conflict check above) - the file was left unmodified.

One-line summary
------------------
GPIO7 confirmed via WEMOS's own C3 Pico schematic as a real onboard WS2812
NeoPixel (U3, DI tied to net IO7) - tagged led_neopixel; GPIO3 confirmed as
a real jumper-gated battery-voltage-sense pin fed by a 100K/100K divider
(R7/R10 off the TP4054 charger's VBAT rail) - left at default capabilities
with a documented 6.6V corrected reference; GPIO9 confirmed (via an
independent board-owner write-up naming it "Button 9") as the onboard BOOT/
download pushbutton - tagged button; no pins_arduino.h alias conflicts were
found for any of the three pins, so pins_arduino.h was left unmodified.
