Board: Deneyap Kart v2 (ESP32-S3 Dev Module variant, onboard camera + microSD)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill — findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- vendor pins_arduino.h - basis for excluding GPIO1/2/4-7/12-17/38-42 as the
  dedicated onboard camera (CAMSD/CAMSC/CAMD2-9/CAMPC/CAMXC/CAMH/CAMV) and
  microSD card (SDCM/SDCK/SDDA) pins.
- docs.deneyapkart.org Pin-Definitions-dyv2 - basis for GPIO0 "Genel amaçlı
  kullanılan dahili butonun (BUT) bağlı olduğu pindir" (general-purpose
  onboard button) and GPIO46 "Dahili adreslenebilir RGB LED'in bağlı olduğu
  pindir" (onboard addressable RGB LED).

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0: capabilities [digital_input, button] - tagged "button". Confirmed
  onboard GPKEY/BUT button per docs.deneyapkart.org Pin-Definitions-dyv2.
- GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO14, GPIO15,
  GPIO16, GPIO17, GPIO38, GPIO39, GPIO40, GPIO41, GPIO42: excluded (removed
  from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated onboard camera and microSD card
  pins, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO3, GPIO8, GPIO9, GPIO10, GPIO11: capabilities [analog_input/
  analog_input_serial, digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO18, GPIO21: capabilities [analog_input_serial or digital_input,
  digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no tag.
- GPIO43, GPIO44, GPIO45: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output,
  pwm_output] - generic, no tag.
- GPIO46: capabilities [digital_output, pwm_output, led_neopixel] - tagged
  "led_neopixel". Confirmed onboard addressable RGBLED/RGB_BUILTIN per
  docs.deneyapkart.org Pin-Definitions-dyv2.
- GPIO47, GPIO48: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.

Summary: Excluded the dedicated onboard camera and microSD card pins
(GPIO1/2/4-7/12-17/38-42) per vendor pins_arduino.h, tagged GPIO0 as
"button" (onboard GPKEY/BUT button) and GPIO46 as "led_neopixel" (onboard
addressable RGB LED), both confirmed via docs.deneyapkart.org
Pin-Definitions-dyv2.
