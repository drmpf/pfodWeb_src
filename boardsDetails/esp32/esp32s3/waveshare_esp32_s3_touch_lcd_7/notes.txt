Pins considered and NOT tagged:
- GPIO0 (BOOT button, pulls GPIO0 low on press): This is the standard ESP32-S3 strapping pin,
  already excluded from chipGpios in this board's board.json as the dedicated ST7262 LCD "G3"
  data line (WS_LCD_G3 = GPIO0 in pins_arduino.h), and already carries a pinNotes entry "Must be
  HIGH for normal boot". Re-adding it as a "button" capability would reverse a pre-existing,
  clearly-justified exclusion (it drives the display) and would also be dangerous/incorrect since
  toggling it in firmware could conflict with the boot-strap requirement and the LCD data line.
  This is a considered, deliberate NO - not a guess, and not a general-purpose user button in the
  first place (it's a programming-mode entry button, standard on nearly all ESP32 dev boards).
- RESET button: not connected to any GPIO at all - it drives EN/CHIP_PU (hardware reset), so
  there is no GPIO to tag even in principle.
- "PWR"/"CHG"/"DONE" LEDs: confirmed via docs.waveshare.com as fixed battery-charge/power-status
  indicator LEDs, not connected to a software-controllable GPIO (typical of dedicated charge-IC
  status pins, hardwired, not part of the ESP32-S3 pin map at all). No GPIO number associated
  with them anywhere in the sources found. Not tagged - no GPIO to tag, and no evidence they are
  driven by the MCU at all.
- No NeoPixel/WS2812 or discrete RGB LED of any kind was found mentioned in any source for this
  board.

No pins_arduino.h alias (LED_BUILTIN, BUTTON, etc.) exists in this board's pins_arduino.h at all,
so there was no misleading alias to guard against beyond the ones described above.

Summary: NO CHANGE. No confirmable onboard general-purpose user button or onboard
software-controllable LED was found for this board. board.json was not modified.
