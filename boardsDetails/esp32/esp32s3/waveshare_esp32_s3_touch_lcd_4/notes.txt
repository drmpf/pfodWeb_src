PINS CONSIDERED BUT NOT TAGGED
-------------------------------
- BAT_PWR / "PWR button": wired into the SW6106 battery-management IC's SYS_EN
  control line per the wiki FAQ text, not to any ESP32-S3 GPIO. No GPIO exists
  to tag. Not a guess - directly stated by the vendor wiki text that this is a
  power-management-IC function, not a GPIO-readable button.
- Red 0602 SMD "PWR light" LED: per schematic, wired directly between 3V3 and
  GND through R58 (10K) with no transistor/GPIO switching. Hardwired always-on
  power indicator - no GPIO exists to tag. Not a guess.
- RESET/EN pushbutton: tied to the ESP32-S3's CHIP_PU/EN pin, which is a reset
  input, not a GPIO - correctly out of scope for capability tagging (same
  pattern already established on this board's siblings, e.g.
  waveshare_esp32_s3_touch_lcd_185).
- No other candidate LED/button pins were found. No best-guess judgment calls
  were made for anything left untagged above - each was ruled out by explicit
  vendor documentation/schematic evidence.

WHAT WAS TAGGED
----------------
GPIO0 tagged ["digital_input","button"] - confirmed as pushbutton "KEY1" on the
ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-4 V3.0 schematic (standard BOOT/download button, active
low), corroborated by the vendor wiki's own FAQ instructions referencing a
"BOOT" button on this board. Not TX/RX (TX=43, RX=44), so no reservation
conflict. This matches the precedent already set on sibling boards
waveshare_esp32_s3_touch_lcd_185 and waveshare_esp32_s3_lcd_147, which tag a
confirmed BOOT pushbutton the same way. No board.json previously existed for
this board; one was created as a full copy of the esp32s3 chip-level default
board.json with only GPIO0's capabilities changed plus this NOTE.
