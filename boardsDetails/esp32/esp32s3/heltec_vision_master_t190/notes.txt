Board: heltec_vision_master_t190 (Heltec Vision Master T190), chip esp32s3
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill - findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- Vendor pins_arduino.h for this board - basis for excluding GPIO5
  (Vext power-gate), GPIO8-11 (LoRa SPI bus), GPIO12-14 (LoRa RST/BUSY/DIO0),
  GPIO38-40/47/48 (TFT display).
- Vendor schematic HT-VMT190 vmt190V0.5.SchDoc - S3 "USER_KEY" wired to
  GPIO21 with pull-up R22, confirming GPIO21 as the onboard USER_KEY button;
  also shows S2 "BOOT_KEY" as a second physical button wired to GPIO0 (the
  reserved boot-strapping pin); LED1 wired as an automatic battery-charge
  indicator directly to the charger IC (not GPIO-driven); LED2 wired
  directly onto the GPIO0/BOOT_KEY net (not an independently drivable
  output).

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0: left at the plain chip-level default capability set. GPIO0 is also
  a physical button (S2 "BOOT_KEY") per the vendor schematic, but is the
  reserved boot-strapping pin, so it was deliberately left untagged.
- GPIO1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 15, 16, 17, 18, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46: left at the
  plain chip-level default capability set (no board-specific note).
- GPIO5: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated onboard
  Vext power-gate pin, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO8, 9, 10, 11: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated
  onboard LoRa SPI bus, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO12, 13, 14: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated
  onboard LoRa RST/BUSY/DIO0 lines, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO21: tagged ["digital_input", "button"]. Confirmed onboard USER_KEY
  button (vendor schematic HT-VMT190 vmt190V0.5.SchDoc: S3 "USER_KEY" wired
  to GPIO21 with pull-up R22).
- GPIO38, 39, 40, 47, 48: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) -
  dedicated onboard TFT display pins, per vendor pins_arduino.h.

Summary: GPIO21 tagged button (onboard USER_KEY, confirmed via vendor
schematic HT-VMT190 vmt190V0.5.SchDoc); Vext power-gate (GPIO5), LoRa SPI
bus (GPIO8-11), LoRa RST/BUSY/DIO0 (GPIO12-14), and TFT display
(GPIO38-40/47/48) excluded as dedicated hardware; GPIO0 (BOOT_KEY button)
left untagged as the reserved boot-strapping pin; no LED tagged since LED1
is a charger-IC-driven charge indicator and LED2 is wired onto the
GPIO0/BOOT_KEY net rather than an independently drivable output.
