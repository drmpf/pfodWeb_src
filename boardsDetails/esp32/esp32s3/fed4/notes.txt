Board: FED4 (Feeding Experimentation Device, ESP32-S3 Dev Module variant)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill — findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- vendor pins_arduino.h - basis for excluding GPIO10-14 (microSD+display
  SPI bus and display CS), GPIO39/42/45/48 (I2S audio amp), GPIO47 (LDO2
  power-control).
- github.com/KravitzLabDevices/FED4, FED4_Buttons.cpp + Buttons-
  Functionality wiki - confirmed GPIO40 as BUTTON_1/USER_BTN_3.
- FED4_LEDs.cpp + LED-Functionality wiki - confirmed GPIO35 as
  NEOPIXEL_PIN/STATUS_RGB (single status NeoPixel) and GPIO36 as
  RGB_STRIP_PIN (8-pixel NeoPixel strip around the nose-pokes).

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0, GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO8, GPIO9:
  capabilities [analog_input, digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output]
  (GPIO0 without analog_input) - generic, no tag.
- GPIO10, GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO14: excluded (removed from chipGpios
  entirely) - microSD+display SPI bus and display CS, per vendor
  pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO15, GPIO16, GPIO17, GPIO18: capabilities [analog_input_serial,
  digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no tag.
- GPIO21: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO35: capabilities [digital_output, led_neopixel] - tagged
  "led_neopixel". Confirmed onboard single status NeoPixel
  (NEOPIXEL_PIN/STATUS_RGB) per FED4_LEDs.cpp + LED-Functionality wiki.
- GPIO36: capabilities [digital_output, led_neopixel] - tagged
  "led_neopixel". Confirmed onboard 8-pixel NeoPixel strip around the
  nose-pokes (RGB_STRIP_PIN) per FED4_LEDs.cpp + LED-Functionality wiki.
- GPIO38: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO39, GPIO42, GPIO45, GPIO48: excluded (removed from chipGpios
  entirely) - I2S audio amp, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO40: capabilities [digital_input, button] - tagged "button". Confirmed
  onboard user button (BUTTON_1/USER_BTN_3) per FED4 official firmware
  FED4_Buttons.cpp + Buttons-Functionality wiki
  (github.com/KravitzLabDevices/FED4).
- GPIO41: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO43, GPIO44: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output]
  - generic, no tag.
- GPIO46: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO47: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - LDO2 power-control,
  per vendor pins_arduino.h.

Summary: Excluded the microSD+display SPI bus/CS (GPIO10-14), I2S audio amp
(GPIO39/42/45/48), and LDO2 power-control (GPIO47) per vendor
pins_arduino.h; tagged GPIO40 as "button" (onboard user button) and
GPIO35/36 as "led_neopixel" (status NeoPixel and 8-pixel nose-poke
NeoPixel strip), both confirmed via the FED4 official firmware and wiki.
