Conclusion on confirmation strength: GPIO38 (WS2812/NeoPixel RGB LED)
and GPIO0 (BOOT button) are each corroborated by at least three
independent sources - this board's own pins_arduino.h (`PIN_RGB_LED 38`
for the LED), a reseller mirror of Waveshare's own wiki text (spotpear),
and two independently-authored ESPHome hardware configs for this exact
product (devices.esphome.io and ryansch/esphome-config) - so both were
tagged. The official waveshare.com wiki page itself could not be
directly fetched (403 Forbidden) but the pins_arduino.h vendor source
plus the two independent ESPHome configs are considered sufficient
corroborating vendor/community hardware evidence per the task's bar.

TX/RX conflict check: this board's TX=43, RX=44 (from pins_arduino.h).
Neither GPIO0 nor GPIO38 coincides with TX/RX, so no conflict - both
tags apply cleanly and will not be silently discarded by build_boards.js's
TX/RX hard reservation.

Pins considered but NOT tagged:
- GPIO21 (buzzer, per the WebSearch summary only - not independently
  confirmed via a directly-fetched source, and a buzzer is not one of
  the allowed button/LED capability tags in any case). Left untouched
  with its default chip-level capabilities. No guess made.
- GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO41, GPIO42, GPIO45, GPIO46 (the six relay-drive
  outputs, per the WebSearch summary and CNX Software's teardown). These
  are relay-driver control lines (opto/transistor driven), not LEDs or
  buttons, and are explicitly out of scope per this task's instructions
  - left untouched.
- No separate ESP32 hardware "RESET" button was tagged - a physical
  RESET button (if present, mentioned only via inference from generic
  Waveshare board layouts) ties to the chip's EN/RESET pin, not a GPIO,
  so it has no digital_input capability to tag in the first place.

Summary of what was tagged:
- GPIO0 -> ["digital_input", "button"] (BOOT button).
- GPIO38 -> ["digital_output", "led_neopixel", "pwm_output"] (onboard
  WS2812 RGB status LED; pwm_output kept because it was already present
  on this pin in the chip-level default).

variants/esp32/esp32s3/waveshare_esp32_s3_relay_6ch/board.json (full
