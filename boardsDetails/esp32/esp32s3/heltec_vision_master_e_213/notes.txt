- GPIO8, 9, 10, 11: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated

- GPIO18: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - dedicated onboard
  Vext power-gate pin, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO21: tagged ["digital_input", "button"]. Confirmed onboard side button
  (Meshtastic Vision Master hardware docs: "GPIO21 Button (side)"). No
  onboard LED confirmed via vendor docs/schematic, so none is tagged on this
  board.

Summary: GPIO21 tagged button (onboard side button, confirmed via
Meshtastic Vision Master hardware docs); E-ink SPI bus (GPIO1-6), LoRa SPI
bus (GPIO8-11), LoRa RST/BUSY/DIO0 (GPIO12-14), and Vext power-gate (GPIO18)
excluded as dedicated hardware; no LED tagged for lack of confirming
vendor evidence.
