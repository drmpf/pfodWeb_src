  schematic PDF for this exact board). Findings:
    * LED1 = "WS2812B-0807" addressable RGB LED. Its DI (data-in) pin is on net
      "RGB_IO", which is also tied to net "IO38" (ESP32-S3 U3 pin GPIO38). This is a
      single-wire addressable NeoPixel-type LED -> confirms GPIO38 = led_neopixel.
    * Two pushbuttons on the schematic: "Key1" and "Key2".
      - Key1: one pin tied to GND, other pin tied to net "RESET" (same net as R1 10K
        pull-up to 3V3, C16 1uF, and U3's CHIP_PU/EN pin). This is the hardware
        EN/RESET button - it resets the whole chip via the EN pin, it is NOT wired to
        any GPIO, so it cannot be exposed as a pfodWeb digital_input/button capability.
      - Key2: one pin tied to GND, other pin tied to net "IO0" (same net as R2 10K
        pull-up to 3V3 and U3 GPIO0 pin). This is the standard ESP32 BOOT/download
        button, active-low -> confirms GPIO0 = button.
