Board: ESP32-S3-Box (ESP32-S3 Dev Module variant)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill — findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- vendor pins_arduino.h - basis for excluding GPIO0/4-8/45/48 (dedicated
  onboard TFT display), GPIO2/15-17/47 (I2S audio), GPIO8/18 (internal
  Wire1 bus for mic ADC/DAC/IMU/touch), GPIO46 (audio amp power), GPIO3
  (touch IRQ); also source of MUTE_PIN 1 alias.
- espressif/arduino-esp32 pins_arduino.h - confirms MUTE_PIN 1.
- espressif/esp-box hardware overview docs - corroborates GPIO1 as the
  onboard MUTE button.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0, GPIO2, GPIO3, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO8, GPIO15, GPIO16,
  GPIO17, GPIO18, GPIO45, GPIO46, GPIO47, GPIO48: excluded (removed from
  chipGpios entirely) - dedicated onboard TFT display, I2S audio bus,
  internal Wire1 bus (mic ADC/DAC/IMU/touch), audio amp power, and touch
  IRQ, per vendor pins_arduino.h.
- GPIO1: capabilities [digital_input, button] - tagged "button". Confirmed
  onboard MUTE button (MUTE_PIN 1) per espressif/arduino-esp32
  pins_arduino.h and espressif/esp-box hardware overview docs.
- GPIO9, GPIO10: capabilities [analog_input, digital_input, digital_output,
  pwm_output] - generic, no tag.
- GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO14: capabilities [analog_input_serial,
  digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no tag.
- GPIO21, GPIO38, GPIO39, GPIO40, GPIO41, GPIO42, GPIO43, GPIO44:
  capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no
  tag.

Summary: Excluded the dedicated onboard TFT display, I2S audio bus, internal
mic/IMU/touch Wire1 bus, audio amp power, and touch IRQ pins per vendor
pins_arduino.h, and tagged GPIO1 (MUTE_PIN) as "button" — confirmed onboard
MUTE button per espressif/arduino-esp32 pins_arduino.h and the espressif/
esp-box hardware overview docs.
