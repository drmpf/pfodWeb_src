Board: atd35s3 (ArtronShop ATD3.5-S3), chip esp32s3
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill — findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- Vendor pins_arduino.h for this board (cited generically for the TFT
  display/shared SPI bus/microSD and I2S DAC/audio exclusions)
- ArtronShop's official ATD3.5-S3-Full-Test-PIO firmware, src/LVGL.cpp:
  BTN_A_PIN (confirms onboard button) and LED_Y_PIN (confirms onboard
  yellow LED)
- Vendor board dimension drawing, labeling the relevant header pins
  "BTN_A" and "LED_Y"

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO3,10,11,12,13,14,17,18: excluded entirely from chipGpios - dedicated
  onboard TFT display + shared SPI bus + microSD card (per vendor
  pins_arduino.h).
- GPIO45,47,48: excluded entirely from chipGpios - dedicated onboard I2S
  DAC/audio (per vendor pins_arduino.h).
- GPIO4: tagged "button" - confirmed onboard BTN_A pushbutton, active-low
  (digitalRead LOW = pressed), from ArtronShop's official
  ATD3.5-S3-Full-Test-PIO firmware (src/LVGL.cpp, BTN_A_PIN) and the
  vendor board dimension drawing labeling this header pin "BTN_A".
  Capabilities: ["digital_input", "button"].
- GPIO5: tagged "led_low" - confirmed onboard LED_Y (yellow LED),
  active-low (digitalWrite LOW turns it on, HIGH is the initial/off
  state), from the same ArtronShop ATD3.5-S3-Full-Test-PIO firmware
  (LED_Y_PIN) and the vendor board dimension drawing labeling this header
  pin "LED_Y". Capabilities: ["digital_output", "pwm_output", "led_low"].
- GPIO0,1,2,6,7,8,9: no tag - standard capabilities ["analog_input",
  "digital_input", "digital_output", "pwm_output"] (GPIO0 kept as
  ["digital_input", "digital_output", "pwm_output"] without analog_input
  in the source board.json).
- GPIO15,16: no tag - standard ADC2-range capabilities
  ["analog_input_serial", "digital_input", "digital_output", "pwm_output"].
- GPIO21,38,39,40,41,42,43,44,46: no tag - standard capabilities
  ["digital_input", "digital_output", "pwm_output"].

Summary: GPIO3/10-14/17/18 excluded (TFT+SPI+microSD), GPIO45/47/48
excluded (I2S DAC/audio); GPIO4 tagged button (BTN_A), GPIO5 tagged
led_low (LED_Y) - both confirmed via ArtronShop's own firmware source and
the vendor's board dimension drawing.
