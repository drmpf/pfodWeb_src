       pushbutton "Key3" pin 2 (with pull-up R29 / cap C28). Key3 is the
       physical RESET button; not a GPIO so no capability tag applies.
     - LED1 ("Red LED0602") is wired through R6 to pin 9 ("STAT") of the
       battery-charger IC U1 (ETA6098) - a hardwired charge-status
       indicator, not GPIO-driven.
     - LED2 ("Red LED0602") has its cathode tied to GND (confirmed in the
       GND net list) and anode via R3 toward the power rail - a hardwired
       power-on indicator, not GPIO-driven.
     - A third pushbutton "Key1" exists (pin 1 also GND, confirmed in the
       GND net list) but its pin-2 net could not be cleanly isolated from
       the flattened/OCR'd PDF text extraction - the surrounding text
       block was too large and ambiguous to assign a single confident net
       name. Nearby labelled nets on the same schematic sheet include
       "Key_BAT" (-> GPIO6), "BAT_Control" (-> GPIO7) and "BAT_ADC" (->
       GPIO8), which together look like a battery power-latch circuit
       (long-press power button + MCU-held power latch), not a general-
       purpose pushbutton. This is consistent with Key1 NOT being listed
       as one of the two documented "Onboard buttons" in source 3 (only
       RESET and BOOT are listed there).
