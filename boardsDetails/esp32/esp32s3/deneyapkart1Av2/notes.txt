Board: Deneyap Kart 1A v2 (ESP32-S3 Dev Module variant, onboard camera + microSD)
Date: 2026-07-14 (documentation backfill — findings from an earlier audit pass, not new research)

Sources consulted:
- deneyapkart.org/en/docs/deneyap-kart-1a-v2 - basis for GPIO0 "General
  Purpose Button (Pin: D15)" and GPIO48 "Addressable RGB LED (Pin: D9)"
  (single data-pin WS2812-style).
- variants/esp32/esp32s3/board.json (chip-level default) - base chipGpios
  map this board.json is a per-board override of.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO0: capabilities [digital_input, button] - tagged "button". Confirmed
  onboard "General Purpose Button (Pin: D15)" per deneyapkart.org/en/docs/
  deneyap-kart-1a-v2.
- GPIO1, GPIO2, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO15, GPIO16, GPIO17, GPIO38,
  GPIO39, GPIO40, GPIO41, GPIO42: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely)
  - onboard camera data/clock/sync lines (also the SS/MOSI/MISO/SCK bus).
- GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO14: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely)
  - onboard microSD card's SPI bus.
- GPIO3, GPIO8, GPIO9, GPIO10: capabilities [analog_input/analog_input_serial,
  digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, kept as "not
  otherwise dedicated".
- GPIO18: capabilities [analog_input_serial, digital_input, digital_output,
  pwm_output] - generic, kept as "not otherwise dedicated".
- GPIO21, GPIO47: kept as SDA/SCL (shared general I2C).
- GPIO43, GPIO44: kept as TX/RX.
- GPIO45, GPIO46: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output, pwm_output] -
  generic, no tag.
- GPIO48: capabilities [digital_output, led_neopixel, pwm_output] - tagged
  "led_neopixel". Confirmed onboard "Addressable RGB LED (Pin: D9)"
  (single data-pin WS2812-style), per deneyapkart.org/en/docs/
  deneyap-kart-1a-v2.

Summary: Per-board override of the esp32s3 chip-level default — removed the
onboard camera bus (GPIO1/2/4-7/15-17/38-42) and microSD SPI bus
(GPIO11-14), kept GPIO0 (GPKEY button, tagged "button"), GPIO3/8/9/10/18
(not otherwise dedicated), SDA/SCL (47,21), TX/RX (43,44), and tagged GPIO48
as "led_neopixel" (onboard addressable RGB LED) — all confirmed via
deneyapkart.org/en/docs/deneyap-kart-1a-v2.
