Sources consulted (real, external, independently fetched this pass):

  simplified) copy of. Confirmed identical pin roles: TX/RX 43/44,
  SDA/SCL 17/18, camera SIOD/SIOC(shared I2C)/Y9-Y2/VSYNC/HREF/PCLK/XCLK

  10/8/7/6/5 (480x320, 8-bit parallel, 40MHz), SDMMC CMD/CLK/DATA on
  20/9/19, microphone MIC_CLK/MIC_DATA on 0/1. (The real upstream file

  of the same camera/TFT pins; this repo's local copy deliberately
  removes those aliases to avoid double-exposing them, per its own
  existing pfodWeb NOTE - a pre-existing, harmless simplification, not
  something this pass needed to change.)

Per-pin findings (cross-checked directly against the real, confirmed
upstream pins_arduino.h #define values):
- GPIO0, GPIO1: excluded (removed from chipGpios entirely) - onboard
  microphone clock/data (MIC_CLK=0, MIC_DATA=1). CONFIRMED exact match
  against the official upstream file.
- GPIO2, GPIO4, GPIO5, GPIO6, GPIO7, GPIO8, GPIO10, GPIO15, GPIO16,
  GPIO45: excluded - onboard 8-bit parallel TFT data/control lines
  (DC=2, WR=4, D7=5, D6=6, D5=7, D4=8, D3=10, D2=15, D1=16, D0=45).
  CONFIRMED exact match.
- GPIO9, GPIO19, GPIO20: excluded - onboard SDMMC microSD card
  (CLK=9, DATA=19, CMD=20; GPIO19/20 already excluded chip-wide as native
  USB D-/D+ pins on every ESP32-S3 board, independently correct).
  CONFIRMED exact match.
- GPIO10, GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13, GPIO3, GPIO14, GPIO47, GPIO21, GPIO38,
  GPIO39, GPIO40, GPIO41, GPIO42: excluded - onboard DVP camera data/
  clock/sync lines (also the shared camera/TFT SPI-style bus): PCLK=11,
  VSYNC=21, HREF=38, XCLK=40, Y2=13, Y3=47, Y4=14, Y5=3, Y6=12, Y7=42,
  Y8=41, Y9=39 (GPIO10 already counted above as TFT_D3). CONFIRMED exact
  match.
- GPIO17, GPIO18: kept as SDA/SCL (shared general I2C, also used by the
  camera's SCCB interface per the upstream file's I2C_SDA/I2C_SCL/
  SIOD/SIOC defines). Capabilities [analog_input_serial, digital_input,
  digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no tag. CONFIRMED correct to
  keep.
- GPIO43, GPIO44: kept as TX/RX. Capabilities [digital_input,
  digital_output, pwm_output] - generic, no tag. CONFIRMED correct to
  keep (standard UART0, not claimed by any onboard peripheral).
- GPIO46, GPIO48: capabilities [digital_input, digital_output,
  pwm_output] - generic, no tag, not otherwise claimed in the upstream
  file. CONFIRMED correct to keep.
