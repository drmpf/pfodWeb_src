https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.1/ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.1_schematic_diagram.pdf
  SUCCESSFULLY FETCHED (this is the real, board-specific schematic - title block reads
  "ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.1"). Read via the Read tool's PDF text extraction. Findings:
   - Net "IO0"/"GPIO0" (ESP32-S3R8, U7, pin 5) includes pushbutton footprint "Key1" pin 2
     (PIKey102), with Key1's other pin (PIKey101) on the GND net, and pull-up resistor R42 (10K,
     schematic shows "R42 10K" near GPIO0/Key1/Key2 block) - classic active-low BOOT/download
     pushbutton wiring. This confirms GPIO0 = the vendor's "BOOT Button".
   - Net "RESET" includes pushbutton footprint "Key2" pin 2 (PIKey202), R43 (10K pull-up), a
     transistor (auto-program circuit VT1/VT2), and U7 pin 4 = CHIP_PU (the chip's EN/reset pin,
     NOT a GPIO). This confirms Key2 = the vendor's "RESET Button", wired to EN, not to any GPIO -
     cannot be tagged as a pfodWeb "button" capability since it isn't a GPIO pin at all.
   - LED1 (0602 SMD LED) cathode is grouped with resistor R6 pin 1 and U1 (ETA6098 battery
     charger IC) pin 9 = "STAT" (charge-status output of the charger IC) - i.e. LED1 is the
     "Charging Indicator" from the wiki's Onboard Resources list, driven by the charger chip, not
     by any ESP32 GPIO.
   - LED2 (silkscreen "贴片LED 侧面红光 0602" = SMD side-emitting red LED) cathode is on the GND
     net; anode side goes through R13 (2.2K) - consistent with an always-on "Power Indicator" LED
     wired directly across a supply rail, not GPIO-driven.
   - No WS2812/NeoPixel/addressable-RGB component, no "RGB" or "WS2812" net/part label anywhere
     in the extracted netlist text.

PINS/CAPABILITIES CONSIDERED BUT NOT TAGGED
---------------------------------------------
- Key2 / RESET button: tied to U7 pin 4 (CHIP_PU/EN), which is not a GPIO and has no entry in
  chipGpios at all. Definitely not tagged - not a judgment call, this is simply not a GPIO pin.
- LED1 (charge-status indicator) and LED2 (power indicator): both confirmed via schematic to be
  driven by fixed hardware (charger IC STAT output / direct supply rail), not any ESP32-S3 GPIO.
  Not tagged - not a judgment call, no GPIO net involved for either LED.

  I2C GPIO expander "Extend_IO1..8" - these are on a separate I2C-addressed expander chip, not
