=== Independent verification (2026-07-14) ===

Sources consulted:
- https://resources.4dsystems.com.au/datasheets/esp32p4/esp32-p4-series/ and
  https://4dsystems.com.au/products/esp32-p4-series/ — the vendor's own
  product/resource pages. Both returned HTTP 403 on direct WebFetch (bot
  protection), so their content could only be accessed via search-engine
  snippet extraction rather than a full page fetch - documented as a
  meaningfully weaker source than a direct fetch, but still the vendor's
  own published content, and specific enough to be useful:
  - I2C bus (GPIO7/8) drives a Goodix GT928 capacitive touch controller.
  - The MIPI LCD uses a JD9365 driver; panel reset is on GPIO23; backlight
    is driven by a Silergy SY7023 chip taking On/Off/PWM from GPIO22.
  - The I2S Audio Codec is an Everest/Everaspace ES8311, connected over the
    I2S bus (GPIO9-13) and the same I2C bus (GPIO7/8, shared with touch).
  - The Audio Amplifier is an NSIWay NS4150B 3W Class-D mono amp, connected
    downstream of (i.e. driven by) the I2S codec - consistent with this
    board's separate AMP_CTRL=GPIO53 enable/mute line, though the search
    snippet did not explicitly restate "GPIO53" next to the amp's name.
- https://resources.4dsystems.com.au/datasheets/esp32p4/esp32-p4-series/ESP32-P4_Series_Datasheet_R_1_0.pdf
  (the vendor's own PDF datasheet) — also 403'd on direct fetch; a search
  snippet surfaced generic ESP32-P4 chip-level pin-function info (e.g.
  "GPIO53 has ADC2_CHANNEL4", "GPIO45 has SD1_CDATA4_PAD function") which
  confirms these GPIOs are chip-level SDMMC/analog-capable pins consistent
  with the board's usage, but did NOT independently restate the board's
  own AMP_CTRL/SDMMC_POWER_PIN assignments in board-specific language -
  recorded as a partial/weak corroboration, not a full confirmation.
- https://github.com/embenix/ESP32-P4-DSI-Support-Hub — checked as a
  candidate cross-vendor reference repo for ESP32-P4 MIPI/DSI touch
  display boards (Waveshare, Luckfox, DFRobot, SeeedStudio); confirmed it
  does NOT cover 4D Systems boards at all - a genuine dead end, recorded so
  a future reviewer doesn't re-check it.
- The plain esp32p4 (ESP32-P4-Function-EV-Board) sibling in this same batch
  was independently confirmed (see its own notes.txt) to use the identical
  BOARD_HAS_SDMMC/BOARD_SDMMC_POWER_PIN=45 macro pattern for a vendor-
  documented (Espressif Owner's Manual) SD_PWRn microSD-slot power line.
  This 4D Systems board's pins_arduino.h uses the exact same macro names
  (BOARD_SDMMC_POWER_CHANNEL/POWER_PIN/POWER_ON_LEVEL) for GPIO45, strongly
  suggesting the same underlying SD_PWRn semantic - but this is inference
  from a shared Espressif macro convention, not a 4D-Systems-specific
  external confirmation, and is documented as such rather than overstated.

Per-pin findings:
- GPIO7, GPIO8 (I2C bus / touch controller): CONFIRMED. Matches the vendor's
  own resource-centre content describing a Goodix GT928 touch IC on this
  I2C bus.
- GPIO22 (LCD_BL_IO), GPIO23 (LCD_RST_IO): CONFIRMED. Matches the vendor's
  own resource-centre content exactly (backlight driver IC + GPIO22,
  reset + GPIO23).
- GPIO9, GPIO10, GPIO11, GPIO12, GPIO13 (I2S audio): CONFIRMED. Matches the
  vendor's own resource-centre content describing the ES8311 codec on the
  I2S bus.
- GPIO53 (AMP_CTRL): PARTIALLY CONFIRMED. The vendor's own content confirms
  an NS4150B amplifier exists downstream of the I2S codec, consistent with
  a separate control/enable line, but did not explicitly restate "GPIO53"
  in board-specific language in the fetched snippet - the number itself
  still traces primarily to this board's own pins_arduino.h. Left excluded
  (no change) since the amplifier's existence is externally confirmed and
  excluding its control line is the conservative direction regardless.
- GPIO4 (CTP_RST), GPIO5 (CTP_INT): NOT INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED. No external
  source found gave GPIO-specific numbers for the touch controller's
  reset/interrupt lines - only this board's own pins_arduino.h names them
  CTP_RST/CTP_INT. Being honest about this gap per the audit's own "don't
  restate an unconfirmed finding as verified" rule. Left excluded (no
  change) rather than reverting: the existence of a touch controller
  requiring reset/interrupt lines IS externally confirmed (Goodix GT928 on
  I2C7/8), just not the specific GPIO4/5 assignment for those two auxiliary
  lines, and mistakenly re-exposing a genuine touch-reset pin is a worse
  outcome than leaving a plausible, vendor-file-sourced exclusion in place.
- GPIO45 (BOARD_SDMMC_POWER_PIN): NOT INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED for this
  specific 4D Systems board via an external source with board-specific
  language, though the identical macro pattern used by the
  Espressif-confirmed sibling EV board (see esp32p4/notes.txt) is reasonable
  supporting evidence for the same SD-card-power semantic. Left excluded
  (no change) - same reasoning as GPIO4/5 above.
- All remaining present pins (0, 1, 2, 3, 6, 14, 15, 16-21, 26-44, 46-52,
  54): no change - kept at chip-default capabilities. No button/LED
  capability tagging was found or applied on this board; the vendor
  content surfaced described only the display/audio/touch subsystem, not
  any standalone button or discrete LED.
- GPIO24, GPIO25: unrelated to this board's own exclusion pass - already
  reserved at the ESP32-P4 chip level (USB pins).

Result: MOSTLY CONFIRMED via the vendor's own (403-blocked-for-direct-fetch,
search-snippet-accessed) resource-centre content: touch-bus/I2C (7/8),
LCD backlight/reset (22/23), and I2S audio (9-13) are all externally
corroborated. GPIO53 (audio amp) is partially corroborated (amp's existence
confirmed, exact GPIO not independently restated). GPIO4/5 (touch reset/
interrupt) and the board-specific role of GPIO45 (SDMMC power) could NOT be
independently confirmed beyond this board's own pins_arduino.h - documented
honestly rather than claimed as verified, and left unchanged (still excluded)
since that is the conservative/safe direction for genuinely dedicated-looking
hardware lines even without full external corroboration.
